\subsection{Datasets and Annotations}
ICBHI and SPRSound provide CHORD's core joint-training data.
ICBHI labels respiratory cycles as Normal, Crackle, Wheeze, or Both
\cite{rocha2019icbhi}; SPRSound provides seven event categories alongside
the binary Normal/Adventitious task studied here\cite{zhang2022sprsound}.
HF Lung supplies timed phase and abnormal-event annotations for a separate
auxiliary-supervision study and attribute evaluation\cite{hsu2021hflung}.
KAUH provides recording-level sound categories and three filter versions
per patient for external evaluation\cite{fraiwan2021kauh}.
Figure~\ref{fig:dataset_characteristics}(a) summarizes these roles and
annotation units.

\subsection{Acoustic Characteristics}
\label{sec:acoustic_characteristics}
After common 5-s preparation (Section~\ref{sec:annotation_alignment}),
we estimate 80--2000~Hz Welch spectra from globally centered
signals\cite{welch1967spectra}. PCA of spectral centroid, bandwidth,
and peak frequency shows source
structure with partial overlap (Figure~\ref{fig:dataset_characteristics}(b)).
Within compatible Normal/Crackle/Wheeze/Both labels, ICBHI retains higher
signal levels and lower spectral centroids than SPRSound
(Figure~\ref{fig:dataset_characteristics}(c,d)).
Thus, matching labels does not eliminate differences in the input
distributions, motivating assessment of both native-task retention and
external transfer. Summaries aggregate within recording and then patient;
HF uses recording-date proxies.
