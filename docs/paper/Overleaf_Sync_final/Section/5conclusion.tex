\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
\revisiondone\par
CHORD connects heterogeneous respiratory annotations through shared acoustic
attributes and dataset-specific readouts. Joint training supports ICBHI and
SPRSound native tasks with gains and costs relative to single-source models.
Fine annotations remain useful under the evaluated loss weighting, while
readout comparisons show that similar C/W discrimination can accompany
different task accuracy. HF and KAUH extend the assessment beyond the core datasets,
with mixed effects from added HF supervision. These findings support reusing
fine acoustic supervision while evaluating task reconstruction and transfer
separately.
