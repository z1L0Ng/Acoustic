\section{Method}
\label{sec:method}
Our method connects heterogeneous annotations through shared acoustic
attributes, then recovers native task labels from their predictions
(Figure~\ref{fig:method_overview}). This separates the information used to
learn the representation from the rules used to make task decisions.

\subsection{Shared Acoustic Attribute Predictors}
For waveform $x_i$, a BEATs iter3+ AS2M encoder\cite{chen2023beats}
pretrained on AudioSet\cite{gemmeke2017audioset} produces
768-dimensional token features. Mean pooling followed by a
linear projection gives $h_i\in\mathbb{R}^{256}$. Three parallel linear
heads predict abnormality (A), Crackle (C), and Wheeze (W):
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
 p_{ia}&=\operatorname{softmax}(W_a h_i+b_a)_{\rm Abnormal},\\
 p_{ic}&=\sigma(W_c h_i+b_c),\qquad
 p_{iw}=\sigma(W_w h_i+b_w).
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
A uses two logits, whereas C/W each use one. All heads jointly fine-tune
the encoder on ICBHI and SPRSound. C/W are marginal attribute probabilities;
the two-level hierarchy is applied in the decision readout.

\subsection{Learning from Available Annotations}
Partial-label learning distinguishes unobserved targets from observed
negatives\cite{shi2021marginal,zhou2022unknown}.
The alignment in Section~\ref{sec:annotation_alignment} supplies targets
$y_{ik}$ and availability masks $m_{ik}$ for $k\in\{a,c,w\}$.
A mask is one only when the source annotation supports that target.
For batch $B$, let
$B_k=\{i\in B:m_{ik}=1\}$ and $K_B=\{k:|B_k|>0\}$. The
classification objective averages available supervision within each node:
\begin{equation}
\mathcal L_{\rm cls}(B)=\frac{1}{|K_B|}\sum_{k\in K_B}
\frac{1}{|B_k|}\sum_{i\in B_k}\ell_k(p_{ik},y_{ik}),
\label{eq:classification_loss}
\end{equation}
where $\ell_a$ is two-class cross-entropy and $\ell_c,\ell_w$ are binary
cross-entropy. This lets compatible labels share a predictor while masking
unsupported targets. Dataset-homogeneous batches contribute equal numbers
of updates per epoch.

To regularize patient-specific variation, we retain PAFA's patient
cohesion-separation and global patient alignment objectives\cite{jeong2025pafa}:
\begin{equation}
\mathcal L_{\rm core}=\mathcal L_{\rm cls}+50\mathcal L_{\rm PCSL}
                                  +0.0005\mathcal L_{\rm GPAL}.
\end{equation}
A separate attention-based projection of encoder tokens supplies their
features during training; patient identifiers are qualified by dataset.

\subsection{Two-Level Native Task Readouts}
The Normal/Abnormal argmax defines $\hat a$ (ties select Normal), and
$\hat c=\mathbb{I}[p_c\geq\tau_c]$,
$\hat w=\mathbb{I}[p_w\geq\tau_w]$ define attribute decisions.
SPRSound uses $\hat a$ for Normal/Adventitious classification.
ICBHI first assigns Normal when $\hat a=0$; otherwise, two positive
attributes yield Both and a single positive attribute yields its class.
If neither is positive, the larger $p_k-\tau_k$ margin selects Crackle
or Wheeze, with ties assigned to Crackle.

Shared thresholds independently maximize the equal-source mean of
available-target binary F1 on core validation data. Candidates are the
observed probabilities plus 0 and 1; ties select the larger threshold.
Changing the readout while fixing the encoder and attribute scores isolates
decision-rule effects from changes in the learned representation.

\subsection{HF Auxiliary-Supervision Condition}
\label{sec:hf_method}
To examine additional attribute supervision, we retain the architecture
and add HF source-training windows:
\begin{equation}
\mathcal L_{\rm HF\mbox{-}on}=\mathcal L_{\rm core}+0.25\mathcal L_{\rm HF},
\end{equation}
using one batch of 32 HF windows per core update. A window overlapping
D, Wheeze, Rhonchi, or Stridor supplies both C/W targets: each is one
when the corresponding D or Wheeze annotation overlaps, and zero otherwise.
$\mathcal L_{\rm HF}$ averages their node-wise BCE losses.
Gap, empty, and phase-only windows are masked; HF supplies no A target.
The derived negatives assume that an absent attribute annotation denotes
absence within these eligible windows, although uncertain events may be
omitted in the source annotations\cite{hsu2021hflung}.
HF is excluded from threshold fitting, checkpoint selection, and early
stopping; its source-test partition is reserved for evaluation.
