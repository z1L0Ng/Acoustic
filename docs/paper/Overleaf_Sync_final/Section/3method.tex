\section{Method}
\label{sec:method}
\revisiondone\par

CHORD shares abnormality (A), Crackle (C), and Wheeze (W)
supervision across ICBHI and SPRSound, then recovers their native task
outputs (Figure~\ref{fig:method_overview}).

\subsection{Shared Acoustic Representation}
\label{sec:annotation_alignment}
For each mono 16-kHz, 5-s input $x_i$, AudioSet-pretrained BEATs iter3+
AS2M\cite{chen2023beats,gemmeke2017audioset} produces $T_i$
768-dimensional tokens $H_i$.
Mean pooling and a linear projection give $h_i\in\mathbb{R}^{256}$.
Three parallel linear heads predict
\begin{equation}
p_{ik}=\phi_k(W_k h_i+b_k),\qquad k\in\{a,c,w\},
\label{eq:attribute_predictions}
\end{equation}
where $\phi_a$ returns the Abnormal probability from a two-logit softmax,
and $\phi_c,\phi_w$ apply sigmoid to one logit each.
Their outputs form the shared attribute vector
$\mathbf p_i=(p_{ia},p_{ic},p_{iw})$.

\subsection{Annotation-Aware Joint Learning}
\label{sec:joint_supervision}
We use cycle and event labels to learn shared acoustic cues: SPRSound's
fine labels contribute C/W information despite its binary native task.
Both sources jointly fine-tune the encoder and heads.
Annotations supply targets $y_{ik}$ and masks $m_{ik}$,
with $m_{ik}=1$ only for supported attributes, preserving the distinction
between unknown and observed negatives\cite{shi2021marginal,zhou2022unknown}.
ICBHI labels specify A/C/W; for SPRSound, we merge Fine/Coarse Crackle and
use Rhonchi/Stridor for A only. For batch $B$, let
$B_k=\{i\in B:m_{ik}=1\}$ and $K_B=\{k:|B_k|>0\}$. Then
\begin{equation}
\mathcal L_{\rm cls}(B)=\frac{1}{|K_B|}\sum_{k\in K_B}
\frac{1}{|B_k|}\sum_{i\in B_k}\ell_k(p_{ik},y_{ik}),
\label{eq:classification_loss}
\end{equation}
where $\ell_a$ is cross-entropy on $(1-p_{ia},p_{ia})$ and
$\ell_c,\ell_w$ are binary cross-entropy.

To account for patient-level acoustic variation, we retain PAFA's patient
cohesion-separation (PCSL) and global patient alignment (GPAL)
losses\cite{jeong2025pafa}. Their patient-based grouping accommodates
heterogeneous class labels. PCSL promotes within-patient cohesion and
between-patient separation; GPAL limits centroid dispersion. Both use a
separate attention-based projection $z_i=g(H_i)$, giving
\begin{equation}
\mathcal L_{\rm core}=\mathcal L_{\rm cls}
 +\lambda_p\mathcal L_{\rm PCSL}+\lambda_g\mathcal L_{\rm GPAL},
\label{eq:core_objective}
\end{equation}
with regularization weights $\lambda_p$ and $\lambda_g$.

\subsection{Native Readouts and Dataset Extension}
\label{sec:native_readouts}
\label{sec:hf_method}
Let $\hat a_i$ be the Normal/Abnormal argmax (ties select Normal),
$\hat c_i=\mathbb{I}[p_{ic}\geq\tau_c]$, and
$\hat w_i=\mathbb{I}[p_{iw}\geq\tau_w]$.
SPRSound uses $\hat a_i$ for Normal/Adventitious classification.
For ICBHI, $\hat a_i=0$ yields Normal; otherwise, two positive attributes
yield Both and one yields its class. If neither is positive, the larger
$p_{ik}-\tau_k$ margin selects Crackle or Wheeze, with ties selecting Crackle.
Thresholds use core validation (Section~\ref{sec:protocol}).

The same attributes support additional supervision and external evaluation.
In a separate condition, HF source-training windows supply C/W targets,
mapping its discontinuous adventitious-sound label D to C. Eligibility and
derived negatives are defined in Section~\ref{sec:protocol}.
Their masked BCE yields $\mathcal L_{\rm HF}$, giving
\begin{equation}
\mathcal L_{\rm HF\mbox{-}on}=\mathcal L_{\rm core}
 +\lambda_{\rm HF}\mathcal L_{\rm HF},
\label{eq:hf_objective}
\end{equation}
where $\lambda_{\rm HF}$ weights auxiliary supervision; HF adds no A or
PAFA loss. HF source-test evaluates intervals from C/W window scores
and recordings from their maxima. KAUH averages probabilities across each
patient's filter versions before binary/four-class decoding of compatible
labels. With models and thresholds fixed, these evaluations assess
core-model transfer and the effects of HF supervision.
