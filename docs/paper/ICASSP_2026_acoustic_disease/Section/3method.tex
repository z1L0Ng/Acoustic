\section{Method}
\label{sec:method}
\subsection{Shared Acoustic Representation and Attributes}
Figure~\ref{fig:method_overview} separates the acoustic input path from the
annotation path. We initialize the encoder with BEATs iter3+ AS2M
\cite{chen2023beats} and fine-tune it jointly on the two datasets.
Its 768-dimensional token features are mean-pooled and linearly projected to
$h_i\in\mathbb{R}^{256}$. Three parallel linear predictors produce
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
 p_{ia}&=\operatorname{softmax}(W_a h_i+b_a)_{\rm Abnormal},\\
 p_{ic}&=\sigma(W_c h_i+b_c),\qquad
 p_{iw}=\sigma(W_w h_i+b_w).
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
The abnormality predictor has two logits; each attribute predictor has one.
All three update the shared representation. The two-level structure is used
for task decisions: the C/W probabilities are marginal predictions, with
no explicit parent--child probability-consistency constraint.

\subsection{Learning from Available Supervision}
For sample $i$ and node $k\in\{a,c,w\}$, $m_{ik}=1$ when its source
annotation supports target $y_{ik}$. For batch $B$, let
$B_k=\{i\in B:m_{ik}=1\}$ and $K_B=\{k:|B_k|>0\}$. The implemented
classification objective averages over available samples within each node:
\begin{equation}
\mathcal L_{\rm cls}(B)=\frac{1}{|K_B|}\sum_{k\in K_B}
\frac{1}{|B_k|}\sum_{i\in B_k}\ell_k(p_{ik},y_{ik}),
\label{eq:classification_loss}
\end{equation}
where $\ell_a$ is two-class cross-entropy and $\ell_c,\ell_w$ are binary
cross-entropy. Dataset-homogeneous batches contribute equally many updates
per epoch. Thus supported labels from either source train the shared
attributes without requiring every sample to supervise every node.

We retain PAFA's patient cohesion-separation and global patient alignment
objectives\cite{jeong2025pafa}:
\begin{equation}
\mathcal L=\mathcal L_{\rm cls}+50\mathcal L_{\rm PCSL}
                       +0.0005\mathcal L_{\rm GPAL}.
\end{equation}
An auxiliary attention-based projection supplies their features, with patient
identifiers qualified by dataset. This auxiliary projection is used during
training and does not enter the native classification readouts.

\subsection{Two-Level Native Task Decisions}
The Normal/Abnormal argmax defines $\hat a$; its ties select Normal.
Attribute decisions are $\hat c=\mathbb{I}[p_c\geq\tau_c]$ and
$\hat w=\mathbb{I}[p_w\geq\tau_w]$. SPRSound directly uses $\hat a$ for
Normal/Adventitious classification. ICBHI assigns Normal if $\hat a=0$.
For an abnormal decision, two positive attributes yield Both and one
positive attribute yields its corresponding class. If neither is positive,
the larger $p_k-\tau_k$ margin selects Crackle or Wheeze, with ties assigned
to Crackle.

For the reported runs, the shared C/W thresholds independently maximize the
equal-dataset mean of eligible-node binary F1 on internal validation data.
Candidates are the observed probabilities together with 0 and 1; exact ties
select the larger threshold. This readout returns task labels from the same
attribute scores. Its contribution can therefore be studied separately from
representation learning by fixing the scores and changing only the decision
rule.
