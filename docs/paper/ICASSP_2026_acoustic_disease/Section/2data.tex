\section{Data and Shared Supervision}
\label{sec:data_alignment}
\subsection{Native Prediction Units and Targets}
The core study uses two presegmented classification tasks. ICBHI provides
respiratory cycles labeled Normal, Crackle, Wheeze, or Both
\cite{rocha2019icbhi}; Both denotes the presence of the two attributes within
the cycle. SPRSound provides event annotations, with Task1-1 asking whether
an event is Normal or Adventitious\cite{zhang2022sprsound}. Each native unit
receives one prediction. This task definition preserves cycle and event
labels without introducing a new temporal-localization objective.

We use the ICBHI official recording split: 4,142 training and 2,756 test
cycles. The test supports are 1,579 Normal, 649 Crackle, 385 Wheeze, and
143 Both. This official split is not strictly patient-held-out.
SPRSound evaluation uses the BioCAS2022 official inter-subject partition:
1,429 events, comprising 1,040 Normal and 389 Adventitious examples.
Its intra-subject test and seven-class Task1-2 are separate endpoints.

\subsection{Shared Attributes and Annotation Coverage}
Let $a$, $c$, and $w$ denote abnormality, Crackle, and Wheeze.
ICBHI labels determine all three targets. Compatible SPRSound event labels
supply the same targets, with Coarse and Fine Crackle merged at the attribute
level. Rhonchi and Stridor supervise abnormality; their C/W targets are
left unavailable in our mapping. An unavailable target supplies neither
positive nor negative classification supervision.

The retained attributes must be sufficient to express the selected task.
They express ICBHI's four categories and SPRSound's binary target, but do
not retain the Coarse/Fine Crackle distinction required for SPRSound
seven-class classification. Native-task preservation here refers to the
specified units and category definitions; performance retention is assessed
against single-task references.

\subsection{Input Construction}
Recordings are converted to mono and resampled to 16~kHz. The inherited
pipeline applies a 1/16-s linear fade at recording boundaries before
extracting each annotated unit. Units longer than 5~s retain their first
5~s; shorter units are repeated to 5~s and receive an end fade.
The two datasets therefore share an input format while retaining their
original supervision units. This preprocessing does not establish that every
long unit's acoustic events are retained.
