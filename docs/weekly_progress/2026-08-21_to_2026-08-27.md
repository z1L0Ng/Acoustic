- Completed R0/N1/A1/L1/L2 and retained sample identity, raw ground truth,

- Closed the direct-comparison boundaries for ICBHI official recording split
  and SPRSound BioCAS2022 inter Task1-1.

## Interpretation

- SPRSound is consistently above 90 and is not the current blocking dataset.
- Peak normalization, gain/noise augmentation and imbalance-aware loss do not
  provide a metric-independent improvement across both datasets.
- Focal loss shifts the operating point toward ICBHI abnormal sensitivity while
  reducing specificity and SPRSound performance.
- The old bits-only readout ignored the trained Level1 Normal/Abnormal node and
  exaggerated ICBHI Normal false positives. The hierarchical diagnostic repairs
  much of the specificity loss but remains below the strongest direct ICBHI
  BEATs literature references.
- The evidence does not yet establish a single unified winner or explain the
  cross-dataset conflict mechanistically.
