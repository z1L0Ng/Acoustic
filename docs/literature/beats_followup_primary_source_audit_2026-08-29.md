# BEATs and Respiratory-Sound Follow-up Work: Primary-Source Comparison and Reproduction Boundary

**Audit date:** 2026-08-29
**Status:** paper-ready source audit; no experiment or new local performance evidence
**Scope:** BEATs, PAFA, the fixed-encoder EMBC evaluation, SPA, Resp-Agent, and the OPERA-benchmark BEATs evaluation
**Out of scope:** model/data download, execution, author contact, Notion changes, and Git operations

## Evidence vocabulary

- **Verified Source Fact:** directly observable in a primary paper, official proceedings page, or official repository.
- **Paper Claim:** a result reported by the paper authors; it is not a local reproduction.
- **Proposed Clean Protocol:** a leakage-reduced comparison contract proposed for this project; it is not the author protocol and has not been executed here.
- **Local Diagnostic:** an already-existing project-side diagnostic used only to motivate a protocol question. It is not a paper result or a clean reproduction.
- **Interpretation:** an evidence-bounded explanation that is not established causally.
- **HOLD:** the primary sources do not resolve the item, or the item requires a separate management or execution decision.

### 2.1 Minimum evidence package for a future authorized run

This is a documentation requirement, not execution authorization:

1. exact source/checkpoint/config identity and license/access boundary;
2. cycle IDs and patient IDs for subtrain, inner validation, and official test;
3. per-epoch training and inner-validation history, with `official_test_accessed=false` before selection;
4. selected epoch and frozen selection metric;
5. one terminal official-test prediction export per seed, including confusion, Sp, Se, Score, and per-class support/recall;
6. separate labels for `author-faithful / official-test-selected` and `clean / validation-selected`.

## 3. Paper-ready English draft

### Related Work

[BEATs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.09058) learns general-purpose audio representations through iterative acoustic tokenization and masked discrete-label prediction. Its original study did not report respiratory-dataset results, but subsequent work has evaluated BEATs under several materially different adaptation regimes. [PAFA](https://www.isca-archive.org/interspeech_2025/jeong25_interspeech.html) fully fine-tuned BEATs_iter3+ AS2M for ICBHI cycle-level four-class classification and reported an ICBHI Score of 63.49±1.08 with cross-entropy alone and 64.84±0.60 after adding patient-aware cohesion-separation and global-alignment losses. A [fixed-encoder evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.18004) instead combined BEATs_iter3 with a target-supervised four-block Transformer and reported 57.31±1.40 on ICBHI. [Resp-Agent](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.15909) used frozen BEATs features within a substantially broader multimodal system that incorporated a trainable projector, Longformer, text, HF Lung and SPRSound pre-training, target-supervised ICBHI fine-tuning, and generative data balancing. [OPERA-benchmark studies](https://www.isca-archive.org/interspeech_2025/niizumi25_interspeech.html) further evaluated frozen BEATs with linear probes on disease-label tasks derived from ICBHI and KAUH, rather than on ICBHI cycle-level adventitious-sound classification or KAUH native labels. These studies establish BEATs as a relevant reference backbone, but they do not constitute a single protocol-matched comparison.

### Limitations and comparison boundary

The strongest direct ICBHI comparator also requires a selection caveat. In the [released PAFA training code](https://github.com/wa976/PAFA/blob/main/main.py), the official ICBHI test loader is evaluated after each epoch and the checkpoint with the highest test Score is retained. We therefore treat the reported PAFA and BEATs+CE values as test-selected paper references rather than leakage-free validation estimates. In addition, our scoped primary-source search found no BEATs result matching the SPRSound BioCAS2022 official inter-patient event tasks, the native temporal and observed-positive-only interpretation of HF Lung, or patient-grouped KAUH raw-nine evaluation with B/D/E recordings retained as views of the same patient. The closest SPRSound evidence used 2,683 recordings rather than official-inter events, while the closest KAUH evidence used an Obstructive-versus-Healthy AUROC task. Consequently, these results should remain dataset-specific background and should not be pooled or presented as direct evidence for cross-dataset label harmonization.

## 4. Why the Core-2 ICBHI diagnostic is lower: claim–evidence map

### Local evidence boundary

The existing Core-2 reference is a full-BEATs, joint ICBHI+SPRSound run with 4-s windows, 2-s stride, seed 42, native-unit batch 8, 50 epochs / 70,200 updates, and validation-only checkpoint selection. Its R0 objective uses the shared `Level1`, `Crackle`, and `Wheeze` hierarchy with CE+BCE and no normalization or augmentation. The previously computed ICBHI hierarchical readout is Sp 67.89, Se 42.91, and Score 55.40. It reconstructs flat four from saved hierarchy outputs and thresholds and is explicitly a **test-informed Local Diagnostic**, not a primary local result, clean reproduction, or paper claim. The project's SPRSound Task1-1 value is kept in its native task and is not pooled with ICBHI.

| Candidate factor | Verified external source facts | Existing local diagnostic evidence | Interpretation | Claim ceiling / evidence still needed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Training-target and readout mismatch** | PAFA BEATs+CE directly optimizes one flat-four softmax with CE. PAFA's reported score is computed from that direct four-class output. | Core-2 jointly optimizes a shared hierarchy (`Level1`, `Crackle`, `Wheeze`) across ICBHI and SPRSound, then reconstructs flat four post hoc. The 55.40 readout increases specificity relative to the earlier bits-only decoder but lowers sensitivity. | The shared hierarchy does not directly optimize four-class competition or ICBHI Score. Thresholded reconstruction can move the operating point toward Normal and may be especially costly for Wheeze/Both and abnormal sensitivity. This is the strongest protocol-level explanation. | **Interpretation, not causal attribution.** A direct flat-four BEATs+CE reproduction under clean selection is required before attributing the gap to the hierarchy. Per-class confusion/recall and matched direct-head versus hierarchy evidence are needed. |
| **2. Window, padding, aggregation, and source-exposure mismatch** | PAFA uses one fixed 5-s input per cycle, repeat-padding short cycles and truncating long cycles; it trains only on ICBHI with batch 32 for 100 epochs and EMA beta 0.5. | Core-2 uses overlapping 4-s/2-s windows, non-repeat padding for short inputs, window aggregation, native-unit batch 8, 50 epochs, and joint ICBHI+SPRSound training. | Brief crackles or wheezes may be split across windows or diluted during aggregation, and joint source sampling changes the number and composition of ICBHI gradients. Repeat padding also preserves short-cycle content differently from zero padding. | **Plausible mechanism only.** Do not claim any one component caused the gap. The author-faithful BEATs+CE baseline is the minimum aggregate test; a large window/sampling grid is not justified before that baseline is resolved. |
| **3. Model-selection and statistical mismatch** | The PAFA repository evaluates the official test loader every epoch, retains the maximum-Score checkpoint, and reports five-seed statistics. | Core-2 selects on held-out validation with no official-test access during training and currently provides one seed for this diagnostic; the hierarchical decoder itself was examined post hoc on saved test predictions. | PAFA's paper number can contain test-selection optimism, whereas Core-2 bears a stricter checkpoint-selection constraint. Five-seed averaging and a single-seed local diagnostic also have different uncertainty. This protocol inequivalence is verified, although its numerical contribution is unknown. | It is safe to state that the comparison is selection-incompatible; it is **not** safe to subtract 55.40 from 63.49 and call the 8.09-point difference a method effect. Clean five-seed BEATs+CE is needed to estimate the selection-adjusted gap. |

### Decision implied by the map

The single deadline-critical baseline is **BEATs+CE under the author input/model/loss contract, with a clean patient-grouped inner-validation selection receipt**. An author-faithful official-test-selected receipt may be retained only to explain the paper target. PAFA losses should follow only if direct BEATs+CE parity is understood. The fixed-encoder EMBC row, SPA, Resp-Agent, and OPERA disease tasks are useful literature references but do not answer the same protocol question and are not priority reproductions.

## 6. Citation-safe summary

- Do not describe PAFA's 63.49/64.84 as clean validation-selected estimates.
- Do not describe the fixed-encoder EMBC experiment as zero-shot; it trains a target-supervised task network.
- Do not compare EMBC's 2,683-recording SPRS result with BioCAS official-inter event results.
- Do not describe Resp-Agent as a BEATs-only or zero-shot ICBHI method.
- Do not use OPERA T7/T10 as evidence for ICBHI flat four or KAUH raw nine.
- Use “no task-compatible result was found in the scoped primary-source audit,” not an absolute claim that no such work exists.
- Keep the Core-2 55.40 value labeled as a test-informed post-hoc diagnostic and never pool it with SPRSound, HF Lung, or KAUH.
